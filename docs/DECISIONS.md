# Decision Log

Engineering decisions for this project. Every entry is the engineer's call; AI drafts the
record. Other documents cite `DEC-00N` by ID and do not restate the reasoning.

Impact: **low** · **medium** · **high**. High-impact touches public API behaviour, security,
data retention, or the data model, or is hard to reverse, and requires recorded sign-off before
implementation. Uncertain defaults to high.

Entries marked *reconstructed* were recorded after the fact rather than at the moment of the
decision, and say so — a record that hides its own provenance is worth less than one that
admits it.

### DEC-001 · &lt;title&gt; <!-- YYYY-MM-DD · impact: low | medium | high -->

**Open question:** One line. If it takes a paragraph, it is more than one decision — split it.

**Options:** A — what it costs, not only what it offers. B — likewise. An option nobody would
choose is not an option; it is a prop that makes the preferred answer look inevitable.

**Discarded:** What was considered and not offered, and why. Omitting an option is a decision
made silently, so it is recorded rather than left out.

**Decision:** What the engineer chose, named explicitly. A recommendation is not a decision.

**Why:** The reasoning that makes it defensible six months later.

**Cost:** What this gives up. An entry with no cost is usually an entry that has not been
thought through.

**Sign-off:** Engineer, date — required for high impact, before implementation.
